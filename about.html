Who We Are

CM Real Estate is a real estate organization based in Kampala, Uganda. We specialize in helping individuals and businesses find suitable properties.

Our organization deals in property sales, house rentals, land transactions and property management in different parts of Uganda.

Not only serving our customers, we are committed to providing reliable, affordable and professional real estate solutions to our customers.

To become a trusted real estate organization in Uganda and beyond.
